The Mad Scientist Armature is built on the work of other open-source makers.
The repository's root [MIT license](LICENSE) covers my (Joseph Ugoretz's) original
contributions, **not an automatic relicensing of third-party material**.
Existing copyright, attribution, and license notices in source files should
be retained.

## Uncanny Eyes — Phillip Burgess / Adafruit

- Project: [Adafruit Uncanny Eyes](https://github.com/adafruit/Uncanny_Eyes)
- Tutorial: [Animated Electronic Eyes](https://learn.adafruit.com/animated-electronic-eyes-using-teensy-3-1/overview)
- Reused material: core eye-animation algorithms and some original eye designs,
  principally visible in `mad_scientist_eye_modules/eye_functions.ino` and
  `mad_scientist_eye_modules/data/`.
- Licensing: the source identifies the work as MIT-licensed. Preserve its
  existing attribution and license notices. The standard MIT license text
  appears in the repository's root `LICENSE`, but its copyright line covers
  my contributions only; original Adafruit notices remain applicable.

The original source credits **Paul Stoffregen** for SPI FIFO insight and
**David Boccabella (Marcwolf)** for the inspiring servo/OLED eye concept.
Those acknowledgments are retained here as well.

- Library: [TFT_eSPI](https://github.com/Bodmer/TFT_eSPI)

## 240 × 240 eyes — Florian Sternke (`dalori`)

- Project: [ESP32 Uncanny Eyes Halloween Skull](https://github.com/dalori/ESP32-uncanny-eyes-halloween-skull)
- License: [MIT, copyright © 2023 Florian Sternke](https://github.com/dalori/ESP32-uncanny-eyes-halloween-skull/blob/main/LICENSE)

Sternke's dual-GC9A01 project and its large eye assets were an important
reference for this hardware and the earlier Halloween goggles. At least the
`default_large.h` design was selected during development. **A complete
file-by-file comparison has not established which current files, if any, are
copied directly from this repository.** If distributing substantial portions
of Sternke's files, retain his original MIT copyright and permission notice.

## Eye graphics: provenance still being checked

`mad_scientist_eye_modules/data/` contains both older 128-pixel eye assets
and numerous 240-pixel `*_large.h` files. Some are clearly related to the
Adafruit designs; others have only generated headers (for example,
`Generated by teensy_eye_convert_v2.py`) and do not identify the underlying
artist or the source image's license. `blueFlameMoD_large.h` is identified in
`config.h` as a local modification.

**Do not assume that all graphics are covered by this repository's MIT
license.** The original artwork's creator and license should be identified
for each distinct design, particularly the large and unusual designs. A
conversion script does not itself confer permission to redistribute the
underlying artwork. Until that review is complete, treat the provenance and
redistribution permissions of unidentified graphics as unresolved.

## Other libraries

The sketches also depend on separately installed libraries, including
[Adafruit NeoPixel](https://github.com/adafruit/Adafruit_NeoPixel) and
TFT_eSPI. Those dependencies retain their own licenses. This repository does
not claim authorship of the libraries.
